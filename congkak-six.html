Responsibility Congkak

Each of your six holes holds 9 seeds and guards one responsibility to clients. Pick a hole and answer its question. Read carefully: several answers will sound reasonable, but only one is fully ethical. Get it right and you sow 3 seeds into your rumah. Get it wrong and 3 seeds drop across into the temptation above it, then into the conflicts store.
